IRS Qualification Requirements

You must meet BOTH requirements to claim this deduction

🎯
Regular & Exclusive Use
The space must be used ONLY for business — regularly and exclusively. A guest room that doubles as an office does NOT qualify. The space must be dedicated solely to your business.
🏢
Principal Place of Business
Your home office must be your principal place of business, OR where you meet clients/customers regularly, OR a separate structure used for business (like a studio or workshop).
👤
Self-Employed Only
W-2 employees working from home can NO LONGER claim this deduction (eliminated in 2018). This deduction is only available to self-employed individuals and business owners.
📝
Two Calculation Methods
The IRS offers two ways to calculate your deduction — the Simple Method (easy, capped at $1,500) and the Actual Expense Method (more work but often larger deduction).

Method 1 — Simplified Method

Easy calculation · IRS rate $5/sq ft · Maximum 300 sq ft · Max deduction $1,500

💡 How it works: Multiply your office square footage by $5.00 (IRS 2025 rate). Maximum office size is 300 square feet, so the maximum deduction is $1,500. No need to track actual home expenses — simple and fast!

Method 2 — Actual Expense Method

More detailed · Often larger deduction · Requires recordkeeping

💡 How it works: Calculate your business use percentage, then apply it to your actual home expenses. More recordkeeping required but often results in a significantly larger deduction — especially for homeowners!
